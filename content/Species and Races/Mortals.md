Mortals is a general term used to describe all mortal beings on Etaarin.

This includes but is not limited to: Humans, Dwarves, Elves, Goblins, Gnomes, Kobolds, Orcs, Goliaths, and Halflings.

When mentioned in creation Myths or other general documents it may also refer to all living life including fish, animals, birds, so on and so forth.
