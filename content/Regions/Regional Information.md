## [[Sandspite]]: 

Sand Spite's landscape is a mountainous desert, very little natural water is on the surface of the land. Massive mesa's shoot out of most of the regions central valley, making some parts of the region extremely cold, due to the lack of sunlight. 

In the Age Of Mortals Sand Spite was ruled by a Human/Tabaxi group called "The Redane Collective" They built a grand city in the desert above a underground water source, they used this as a hub for trading and eventually constructed "The Grand Library" a gargantuan library that stored knowledge and secrets from every passing Caravan. During the age of Oblivion, Sand Spite went through what is now known as "The Great Collapse",  mesa's toppled, The mountains slid from their peaks, and The Redane Collective was buried beneath the endless sands.  

Sand Spite's ownership is currently disputed by a number of dragons, Bandit clans, and state sponsored treasure hunters all of which are searching for treasure lost during "The Great Collapse". The region is without a formal leadership system, and lawlessness is the status quo. 

## [[Goldgrass]]:
Gold Grass's Landscape is one of idyllic rolling fields and long stretches of flatlands ideal for farming. Bountiful rivers flow from the mountains into the northern and eastern seas, bringing with them large groups of fish. 

Goldgrass is majority human dictatorship ruled by a being known as "Azbel, The Divine" they have ruled gold grass for almost 2000 years, coming into prominence just after the end of The Age of Oblivion.  

"Azbel, The Divine" is of a unknown origin, they are never seen in public, and almost all other particulars are unknown to the general public. They exert their will through a secret police called "The Golden Hand", which dispenses punishments in a very draconic¹  fashion.  

Goldgrass is an authoritarian state and uses a rigid class system to supress the general population. The working class has little to no rights and is subjected by the noble class who enforce the status quo set by Azbel.  Immigration is strictly enforced and tourism is regulated to only allow rich trading partners access to the country.  

The countries main export is produce and food goods. The aptly named Capital "Prosperity" is quite affluent, with many of the aforementioned noble houses having their headquarters in the city 

¹Draconic  as in the English meaning of the word, not "Draconic" in origin. 

## [[Poxmire]]:
Poxmire is a dangerous marsh region separating Ekantus and Lacastor, Its landscape is mostly flat with massive  Swamps, dotting the sickly forests.  

Poxmire is home to many unsightly beasts, though they may live in the murky swamps, they will often hunt in nearby regions due to the relatively small population of Poxmire. There are a few small stilted villages that populate the swamps.  

Poxmire is largely controlled by an outlaw group known as "The Green Serpents". Their main export is Hashish, A sticky green smokable substance that can be found in almost any major city throughout Etaarin. The Green Serpents are responsible for producing and export up to 95% of Hashish, as the plant grows freely in Poxmire. The Green Serpents have a home base of operations known as "Big Stick" a stilt city somewhere in the centre of Poxmire. They also provide security to many of the small villages in Poxmire, in return for the production of Hashish. 

Poxmires history is rather uninteresting, they are rarely a target of invasion due to the difficulty of moving large amounts of ground troops into the area and due to the lack of natural resources in the region (disregarding the production of Hashish, which many countries view as an illegal substance.) 

  
  

## [[N.U.A & S.U.A]] (Northern & Southern United Archipelagoes):

N.U.A and S.U.A stand for Northern United Archipelagoes, and Southern United Archipelagoes respectively. Though they are also referred to as Nua, and Sua by some.   

The N.U.A and S.U.A are 2 collectives of independent Islands on the west coast of Etaarin, Each island has its own government and rules but they have trade and immigration agreements within their respective collectives. 

The N.U.A and S.U.A have an uneasy relationship with the mainland. The S.U.A has offered membership status to the island of Palatia, which is a puppet state of Regalta. Accusations of foul play in Palatia's political independence has strained the relationship between the S.U.A and Regalta. Meanwhile the N.U.A has been accused by the nation of Lacastor of arming rebels on the Island of Altren. 

The N.U.A and S.U.A trade with each other freely but have been unable to reach an agreement on a singular formation due to political differences between the extremist's of both collectives. 

## [[Zlatya]]:

Zlatya's landscape is one of sheer cliffs and barren ice, a desolate and unforgiving land. 

The native creatures of Zlatya are extremely hardy, and very sparce, monsters that live in Zlatya  

Zlatya is home to House Nakuma, A dwarven clan with a large city in the centre of the landmass. The Nakuman dwarves are a hardy and violent people, often raiding nearby barbarians and occasionally mounting army's large enough to raid and sack the cities of northerners. 

House Nakuma holds alliances with local some local White Dragons, and the frigid Island nation of Vorkuta.**
