<h3> General Notes: <h3>

Almost everything in this document is subject to change, particularly names of people and places. 

This is a brief description of each major region in Etaarin. Each region will have a detailed document going over its History, Denizens, Geography and Politics at a later date. 

The names of the various regions will differ locally from what can be found in this document and the supplementary map. Some regions are named after the prevailing kingdoms, while others are named based on popular terms given to them by locals.  

If any more information is required (as I'm sure it will be) feel free to reach out to me. 

 ## [[Arrendel]] : 

A land of unrest and civil war, Arrendel has fallen into deep strife and is unlikely to dig itself out in the coming years. 

Ghouls, and bandits scour battlefields for loot and meals. Mercenaries companies sell their services to the highest bidder, Towns rely on depleted town guard regiments to keep them safe. Things are not well in Arrendel, and it doesn't look like they will improve soon.
